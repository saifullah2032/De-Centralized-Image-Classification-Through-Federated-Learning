================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

PROBLEM: Training stops or hangs
SOLUTION: Check all 3 terminals are running. Restart if needed.

PROBLEM: Accuracy not improving
SOLUTION: Normal for first 2-3 rounds. Wait until Round 4-5.

PROBLEM: Web interface shows "Model not found"
SOLUTION: Training must complete at least 1 round first.

PROBLEM: Predictions are all wrong
SOLUTION: Normal with only 20% accuracy. Train more rounds.

PROBLEM: Browser shows "Connection refused"
SOLUTION: Make sure web server is running: python run_web.py

PROBLEM: Visualization doesn't display
SOLUTION: Image saved to models/training_visualization.png - open manually
